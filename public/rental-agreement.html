Terms & Conditions

This Car Rental Agreement is entered into between Ayrton Paul Lohigorry, operating as Route 305 Rental Car / éPure Drive ("Owner/We/Us") and the Renter identified above ("You/Renter"). Both parties agree to the following terms:

1. Definitions

"Agreement" means all terms and conditions herein and any documents you sign at the time of rental. "You/Your" or "Renter" means each person signing this Agreement, any Authorized Driver, and any person or organization to whom charges are billed at your direction. "We/Our/Us" means the Owner identified above. "Authorized Driver" means the Renter and any additional driver we list herein, each at least 21 years of age and holding a valid driver's license; only Authorized Drivers may operate the Vehicle. "Vehicle" means the automobile identified herein and all its tires, tools, accessories, equipment, keys and documents. "Loss of Use" means our lost ability to use the Vehicle while it is damaged or being repaired. "Diminution of Value" means the reduction in the Vehicle's market value after damage and repair.

2. Rental, Indemnity, Personal Property & Warranties

This is a contract for rental only; you may not sublease the Vehicle. We may repossess the Vehicle at your expense, without notice where permitted by law, if it is abandoned or used in violation of law or this Agreement. You agree to indemnify, defend and hold us harmless from all claims, liability, costs and attorney fees arising from this rental or your use of the Vehicle. We are not responsible for loss of or damage to personal property left in or on the Vehicle. We make no warranties, express or implied, regarding the Vehicle, including any warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

3. Condition and Return of Vehicle

Rental of the Vehicle constitutes a bailment for your benefit. You must return the Vehicle to the specified location by the due-in date and time, in the same condition received except for ordinary wear. Our determination of the Vehicle's condition is subject to a final inspection, which may occur after drop-off whether or not the Vehicle is checked in by an employee. Extensions require our prior written approval before the due-in date, and charges continue to accrue until the Vehicle is returned. You must maintain all fluid levels, immediately notify us of any warning lights, overheating, leaks or mechanical issues, and may not perform service or authorize repairs without our prior approval.

4. Responsibility for Damage or Loss; Police Reporting

Regardless of fault, you are responsible for all damage to, loss of, or theft of the Vehicle during the Rental Period from any cause, including collision, weather, terrain, vandalism and acts of God. Your responsibility includes: (a) repair costs, or if the Vehicle is a total loss, its fair market value less salvage; (b) Diminution of Value; (c) Loss of Use at the daily rental rate for each day the Vehicle is unavailable, payable regardless of fleet utilization; (d) an administrative fee based on the damage amount ($50 for damage up to $500, $100 up to $1,500, $200 up to $2,500, and $250 over $2,500); (e) towing, storage, impound and other incidental damages; and (f) all enforcement and collection costs including attorney fees. You must report all accidents, theft and vandalism to us and the police as soon as you discover them.

5. Insurance and Responsibility

We do not provide automatic insurance coverage with this Agreement unless it is specifically purchased and shown on the Reservation Summary. You are fully responsible for any damage, loss, theft, Loss of Use, Diminution of Value, towing and administrative fees, regardless of fault, unless optional coverage has been purchased. If you rely on your own insurance or credit-card benefits, it is solely your responsibility to verify that they apply, and we are not responsible if such coverage is declined. If optional coverage is purchased from us, the deductible is $1,000 for vehicles valued under $35,000 and $2,500 for vehicles valued at $35,000 or more. Coverage is void if this Agreement is breached or if you fail to cooperate in any loss investigation.

6. Prohibited Uses (voids coverage and terminates rental)

The Vehicle shall not be used: (a) by anyone not listed as an Authorized Driver or whose license is suspended; (b) by anyone under the influence of drugs or alcohol; (c) by anyone who gave us false or misleading information; (d) for any illegal purpose; (e) to carry persons or property for hire, or to push or tow anything; (f) in any race, speed test or contest; (g) to teach anyone to drive; (h) to carry hazardous or illegal materials; (i) outside the State of Florida without our prior written approval; (j) on unpaved roads; (k) to carry more persons than there are seat belts, or children without approved safety seats; (l) while the odometer is disconnected or tampered with; (m) while sending or reading a text or email; or (n) smoking or vaping any substance in the Vehicle. ANY PROHIBITED USE AUTOMATICALLY TERMINATES THIS RENTAL AND VOIDS ALL COVERAGE WHERE PERMITTED BY LAW.

7. Accidents & Incidents; Responsibility to Others

You are responsible for all injury, damage or loss you cause to yourself and others, including passengers. You must (a) report all accidents to us and the police as soon as they occur; (b) complete our incident report form; and (c) provide us any legal papers you receive relating to the incident. Failure to report is a material breach and may void optional coverage. We reserve the right to subrogate against you for any amount we must pay to injured or damaged parties. The Vehicle may not be taken into Mexico under any circumstances.

8. Payment and Charges

You permit us to hold a security deposit. You agree to pay all charges including: rental rate, optional services, fuel and refueling fees, government taxes and surcharges, fees to locate or recover the Vehicle, attorney fees and court costs, a 2% per-month late-payment fee (or the maximum permitted by law), a $50 fee for any returned payment, reasonable cleaning fees for excessive mess or smoking evidence, towing and storage charges, key/transponder replacement fees, and a surcharge for returning the Vehicle to a different location or after the due-in date. All charges are subject to a final audit, and you authorize us to correct any errors with your payment-card issuer.

9. Tolls & Traffic Violations

You are liable for all tolls, parking citations, photo-enforcement fees, fines for toll evasion, and other violations assessed against you, us or the Vehicle during the rental period. You authorize us to pay any toll or violation on your behalf and charge you the face value plus applicable taxes and an administrative fee of $35 per occurrence.

10. Fuel Policy

The Vehicle will be returned with at least the same fuel level as at pickup. A refueling fee of $10 plus the cost of fuel will be charged if the Vehicle is returned with less fuel.

11. Mileage

The rental includes the mileage allowance stated in the Reservation Summary. Miles driven beyond the stated allowance will be charged at the overage rate specified (default $0.25/mile), billed at return. Unlimited-mileage agreements are subject to fair use within the rental period.

12. Late Return & Extensions

Vehicles returned more than 2 hours late without an approved extension will be charged an additional day's rental rate for each day or partial day after the due-in time, plus any applicable surcharge for returning the Vehicle to a different location.

13. Cancellation

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before pickup are fully refundable. Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a one-day charge. Once the rental period begins and the Vehicle is made available, charges are governed by the Chargebacks section below.

14. Personal Information; Communications

You agree that we may disclose your information to law enforcement or third parties in connection with enforcing this Agreement, and that we, our assignees, or collection agencies may contact you by phone (including wireless numbers and automated dialing), text, or email at any number or address you provide, to service the account or recover amounts owed.

15. Chargebacks; No Refund for Non-Use

By signing this Agreement and/or accepting the Vehicle, you agree that all rental charges, deposits and fees are valid, earned and non-cancellable once the rental period begins and the Vehicle is made available to you. Claiming the Vehicle was not used, not driven, or returned early does not cancel this Agreement or eliminate your financial responsibility, because the Vehicle was reserved and held exclusively for you. You agree not to dispute or charge back any valid charge. If you initiate a chargeback for a valid charge, it constitutes a material breach and we may pursue recovery of the disputed amount plus administrative, bank-dispute, collection and attorney fees.

16. Smoking; Cleaning & Decontamination Fee

Smoking, vaping, or use of tobacco or cannabis inside the Vehicle is strictly prohibited. If there is any evidence of smoking — odor, ash, residue, burns or stains — a cleaning and decontamination fee of $250 to $500 may be charged to the payment card on file or deducted from the security deposit. You agree this fee is a reasonable estimate of cleaning cost and Loss of Use and is non-refundable.

17. Security Deposit & Payment Authorization

You authorize us to place a hold or collect a security deposit before or at the start of the rental, which may be retained in whole or part for damage, smoking, excessive cleaning, unpaid tolls or violations, late return, extra mileage, fuel shortage, or other charges under this Agreement. The deposit is not a limit on your liability. By providing a payment card you represent that you are the cardholder or are authorized to use it, and you authorize charges processed after the rental period for damage, Loss of Use, tolls, violations, and the fees described in this Agreement, including where a third-party card is used.

18. Damage Pre-Authorization; Evidence of Rental

You authorize us to place a pre-authorization hold and to charge the payment card on file up to the estimated cost of repair, Loss of Use and related fees based on a repair estimate or third-party assessment. You acknowledge that the signed Agreement, photographs and video of the Vehicle and your identification, GPS and telematics data, and time-stamped delivery records may be used as conclusive evidence that the rental service was provided and authorized in the event of any dispute or chargeback.

19. High-Performance Use; Misuse Fee

Burnouts, drifting, racing, speed contests, launch-control abuse, and operation at excessive speed are strictly prohibited. If we determine through GPS, telemetry, video or other reasonable evidence that the Vehicle was misused, a misuse fee of $500 to $1,000 may be charged in addition to any damage or Loss of Use charges.

20. Personal Property; GPS & Telematics

We are not responsible for loss of or damage to personal property left in or on the Vehicle; property left for more than 30 days may be disposed of at our discretion, and you should erase any personal data from the Vehicle's systems before returning it. The Vehicle may be equipped with GPS, telematics, or event-data recorders, and you acknowledge and authorize remote monitoring — including location, odometer, fuel level, and diagnostic data — which we may use to enforce this Agreement. We may disable the Vehicle upon breach of this Agreement, and you agree to inform all drivers and passengers of this provision.

21. Governing Law; Jury Trial Waiver; Miscellaneous

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, with venue in Miami-Dade or Broward County courts. No term may be waived or modified except by a writing signed by us. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties; all prior representations are void. If any provision is held void or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain valid. To the extent permitted by law, you release us from liability for consequential, special or punitive damages, and you and we each waive the right to a trial by jury in any proceeding arising out of this Agreement.

The Renter confirms the Vehicle was received in the condition noted above and agrees to return it in the same or better condition at the agreed time and location.
